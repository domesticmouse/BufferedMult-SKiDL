# KiCad 10 PCB Layout Guide — Eurorack Buffered Multiple

This guide walks through laying out the **Triple 1-to-3 (Normalized to 1-to-6 / 1-to-9) Eurorack Buffered Multiple** PCB in **KiCad 10** using [`buffered_multiple.net`](buffered_multiple.net) generated by [`buffered_multiple.py`](buffered_multiple.py).

---

## 2. Create the Board & Import the Netlist

1. Open **KiCad 10** and either create a new project (`File -> New Project...`) or launch **PCB Editor** (`pcbnew`) directly and save a new board as `BufferedMult.kicad_pcb` in this directory.
2. In **PCB Editor**, select **`File -> Import -> Netlist...`**.
3. Browse to [`buffered_multiple.net`](buffered_multiple.net).
4. Set **Match Method** to **Keep existing symbol to footprint associations** (or **Timestamp / Unique ID** when re-importing — [`build_buffered_multiple()`](buffered_multiple.py) assigns deterministic SKiDL `tag` values so UUIDs remain stable across script regenerations).
5. Click **Load and Test Netlist** (verify `0` errors), then click **Update PCB** and drop the footprints onto the canvas.

---

## 3. Define the Eurorack Board Outline (`Edge.Cuts`)

A 2-column grid of 12 jacks (6 rows × 2 columns) fits cleanly on a single-board **4HP** (`20.0 mm` panel width) Eurorack PCB:

1. Select the **`Edge.Cuts`** layer in the right-hand **Appearance** panel.
2. Set the grid to `0.5 mm` or `1.0 mm`.
3. Use the **Draw Rectangle** tool to draw the board outline:
   - **Height**: `106 mm – 108 mm` (clears standard `110 mm` Eurorack rail lips while providing comfortable spacing for 6 rows of 2 jacks).
   - **Width**: `20.0 mm` (for a 4HP module).

## 4. Component Placement & Layer Assignment

### Front Side (`F.Cu`) — Panel-Facing Side (Jacks & Bulk Capacitors)

Keep **`F.Cu`** free of SMD parts so all factory-assembled SMT components sit on a single side (`B.Cu`), and mount the through-hole jacks (`J2`–`J13`) and low-profile radial bulk capacitors (`C1`, `C2`) on **`F.Cu`**:

1. **Eurorack Jacks (`J2`–`J13`)**:
   - Place the 12 Thonkiconn jacks (`J2`–`J13`) on **`F.Cu`** in a 2-column × 6-row grid (`X = 5.0 mm` and `X = 15.0 mm` relative to the board left edge):

| Order (Row / Col) | Ref | Label / Value | Channel & Function | Associated Passive / Protection on `B.Cu` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 (Row 1 Left) | `J2` | `IN_1` | Ch 1 Input (`TN` normalled to `GND`) | `D3` (`PESD12VL1BA` TVS), `R1` (`100K` pull-down), `R2` (`1K` series to `U1` pin 3) |
| 2 (Row 1 Right) | `J3` | `OUT_1A` | Ch 1 Buffered Output A (`U1B`) | `R3` (`470R` from `U1` pin 7) |
| 3 (Row 2 Left) | `J4` | `OUT_1B` | Ch 1 Buffered Output B (`U1C`) | `R4` (`470R` from `U1` pin 8) |
| 4 (Row 2 Right) | `J5` | `OUT_1C` | Ch 1 Buffered Output C (`U1D`) | `R5` (`470R` from `U1` pin 14) |
| 5 (Row 3 Left) | `J6` | `IN_2` | Ch 2 Input (`TN` normalled to `CH1_BUF`) | `D4` (`PESD12VL1BA` TVS), `R6` (`100K` pull-down), `R7` (`1K` series to `U2` pin 3) |
| 6 (Row 3 Right) | `J7` | `OUT_2A` | Ch 2 Buffered Output A (`U2B`) | `R8` (`470R` from `U2` pin 7) |
| 7 (Row 4 Left) | `J8` | `OUT_2B` | Ch 2 Buffered Output B (`U2C`) | `R9` (`470R` from `U2` pin 8) |
| 8 (Row 4 Right) | `J9` | `OUT_2C` | Ch 2 Buffered Output C (`U2D`) | `R10` (`470R` from `U2` pin 14) |
| 9 (Row 5 Left) | `J10` | `IN_3` | Ch 3 Input (`TN` normalled to `CH2_BUF`) | `D5` (`PESD12VL1BA` TVS), `R11` (`100K` pull-down), `R12` (`1K` series to `U3` pin 3) |
| 10 (Row 5 Right) | `J11` | `OUT_3A` | Ch 3 Buffered Output A (`U3B`) | `R13` (`470R` from `U3` pin 7) |
| 11 (Row 6 Left) | `J12` | `OUT_3B` | Ch 3 Buffered Output B (`U3C`) | `R14` (`470R` from `U3` pin 8) |
| 12 (Row 6 Right) | `J13` | `OUT_3C` | Ch 3 Buffered Output C (`U3D`) | `R15` (`470R` from `U3` pin 14) |

2. **Radial Bulk Electrolytic Capacitors (`C1`, `C2` on `F.Cu`)**:
   - Keep **`C1`** and **`C2`** (`10uF`, `ECEA1EKS100`, `CP_Radial_D5.0mm_P2.00mm`) on **`F.Cu`** so they sit in the `~10.5 mm` space between the PCB and the front panel created by the Thonkiconn jacks (`ECEA1EKS100` is only `7 mm` tall, so it clears the back of the panel easily).
   - Place `C1` and `C2` near the power entry area (for example, between jack bodies or at the top/bottom end of the board adjacent to `J1` and `D1`/`D2`):
     - **`+12V` Bulk Cap (`C1`)**: Pin `1` (`+`, square pad) connects to `+12V`; Pin `2` (`-`) connects to `GND`.
     - **`-12V` Bulk Cap (`C2`)**: Pin `1` (`+`, square pad) connects to `GND`; Pin `2` (`-`) connects to `-12V` (since `GND` is positive relative to `-12V`).

### Back Side (`B.Cu`) — All SMD Parts + Rear THT Power Header

Press **`F`** while hovering over or selecting the following footprints to flip them onto the back layer (`B.Cu`):

1. **All 29 SMD Parts (`U1`–`U3`, `D1`–`D5`, `C3`–`C8`, `R1`–`R15`)**:
   - **Channel 1 Op-Amp (`U1`) & Decoupling (`C3`, `C4`)**:
     - Flip `U1` (`TL074`) to `B.Cu` and place it in the open area between the Channel 1 jack rows (`J2`–`J5`).
     - Place `C3` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U1` pin 4 (`+12V`) and `C4` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U1` pin 11 (`-12V`).
   - **Channel 2 Op-Amp (`U2`) & Decoupling (`C5`, `C6`)**:
     - Flip `U2` (`TL074`) to `B.Cu` and place it between the Channel 2 jack rows (`J6`–`J9`).
     - Place `C5` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U2` pin 4 (`+12V`) and `C6` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U2` pin 11 (`-12V`).
   - **Channel 3 Op-Amp (`U3`) & Decoupling (`C7`, `C8`)**:
     - Flip `U3` (`TL074`) to `B.Cu` and place it between the Channel 3 jack rows (`J10`–`J13`).
     - Place `C7` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U3` pin 4 (`+12V`) and `C8` (`100nF`) immediately adjacent to `U3` pin 11 (`-12V`).
   - **Input Protection & Output Resistors (`D3`–`D5`, `R1`–`R15`)**:
     - Place `D3` (`PESD12VL1BA` in `SOD-323`) and `R1` (`100K`) right next to `J2` (`IN_1`) tip pin (`T`) to `GND`, and route through `R2` (`1K`) into `U1` pin `3`.
     - Place `D4` (`PESD12VL1BA` in `SOD-323`) and `R6` (`100K`) right next to `J6` (`IN_2`) tip pin (`T`) to `GND`, and route through `R7` (`1K`) into `U2` pin `3`.
     - Place `D5` (`PESD12VL1BA` in `SOD-323`) and `R11` (`100K`) right next to `J10` (`IN_3`) tip pin (`T`) to `GND`, and route through `R12` (`1K`) into `U3` pin `3`.
     - Place `R3`, `R4`, `R5` (`470R`) directly inline between `U1` output pins (`7`, `8`, `14`) and the tip pins (`T`) of `J3`, `J4`, `J5`.
     - Place `R8`, `R9`, `R10` (`470R`) directly inline between `U2` output pins (`7`, `8`, `14`) and the tip pins (`T`) of `J7`, `J8`, `J9`.
     - Place `R13`, `R14`, `R15` (`470R`) directly inline between `U3` output pins (`7`, `8`, `14`) and the tip pins (`T`) of `J11`, `J12`, `J13`.
2. **Rear Through-Hole Power Header (`J1`)**:
   - Flip **`J1`** (`IDC-Header_2x05_P2.54mm_Vertical`) onto **`B.Cu`** so the shrouded header protrudes out the **back** of the module (away from the front panel) and its leads are soldered on `F.Cu`.
   - Place `J1` in an open gap between jack rows or near the bottom/center of the board, making sure its through-hole pins clear the bodies of the front-side jacks and bulk capacitors (`C1`, `C2`) on `F.Cu`.
   - Place **`D1`** and **`D2`** (`1N5819WS` in `SOD-323`, SMD on `B.Cu` — or `B5819W` in `SOD-123`, LCSC `C8598` as a larger alternative) right next to `J1` and the through-hole pads of `C1` and `C2`.
   - Add a clear **`-12V / RED STRIPE`** text label on `B.Silkscreen` next to `J1` pins `1` and `2`.

## 5. Configure Net Classes & Design Rules

1. Open **`File -> Board Setup... -> Design Rules -> Net Classes`**.
2. Configure the two net classes defined in [`build_buffered_multiple()`](buffered_multiple.py):
   - **`Power`** (`+12V`, `-12V`, `GND`, `+12V_RAW`, `-12V_RAW`):
     - Track Width: **`0.50 mm`**
     - Clearance: **`0.25 mm`**
   - **`Signal`** (`CH1_JACK_IN`, `CH1_IN`, `CH1_BUF`, `CH1_OP_A..C`, `CH1_OUT_A..C`, `CH2_JACK_IN`, `CH2_IN`, `CH2_BUF`, `CH2_OP_A..C`, `CH2_OUT_A..C`, `CH3_JACK_IN`, `CH3_IN`, `CH3_BUF`, `CH3_OP_A..C`, `CH3_OUT_A..C`):
     - Track Width: **`0.25 mm`**
     - Clearance: **`0.20 mm`**

## 6. Routing & Ground Planes

### 1. Create `GND` Copper Zones (`Ctrl+Shift+Z` / `Cmd+Shift+Z`)
- Draw a filled copper zone assigned to **`GND`** on both **`F.Cu`** and **`B.Cu`** covering the board outline.
- Because all jack sleeve pins (`S`), `J2`'s normalled switch contact (`TN`), `J1` pins `3`–`8`, bulk capacitors `C1` (pin 2) & `C2` (pin 1), decoupling capacitors `C3`–`C8`, TVS diodes `D3`–`D5`, and pull-down resistors `R1`, `R6`, & `R11` connect to `GND`, the copper pours will connect them with low-impedance return paths automatically.

### 2. Route Signal & Power Tracks (`X` Key)
- **Op-Amp Unity-Gain Feedback Loops** (on `U1`, `U2`, and `U3` on `B.Cu`):
  - Bridge adjacent output and inverting-input pins directly at each `SOIC-14` footprint:
    - Pin `1` ↔ Pin `2` (Stage A input buffer feedback)
    - Pin `7` ↔ Pin `6` (Stage B output buffer feedback)
    - Pin `8` ↔ Pin `9` (Stage C output buffer feedback)
    - Pin `14` ↔ Pin `13` (Stage D output buffer feedback)
- **Internal Buffered Bus (`CH1_BUF`, `CH2_BUF`, & `CH3_BUF`)**:
  - Route **`CH1_BUF`** from `U1` pins `1`/`2` to `U1` non-inverting input pins `5`, `10`, and `12`, and extend it down to **`J6` (`IN_2`) pin `TN`** (this normalizes Channel 1's buffered signal into Channel 2 when `IN_2` is unpatched).
  - Route **`CH2_BUF`** from `U2` pins `1`/`2` to `U2` non-inverting input pins `5`, `10`, and `12`, and extend it down to **`J10` (`IN_3`) pin `TN`** (this normalizes Channel 2's buffered signal into Channel 3 when `IN_3` is unpatched).
  - Route **`CH3_BUF`** from `U3` pins `1`/`2` to `U3` non-inverting input pins `5`, `10`, and `12`.
- **Input & Output Paths**:
  - Route `J2` pin `T` (`CH1_JACK_IN`) directly to `D3` (`PESD12VL1BA`), `R1` (`100K`), and `R2` (`1K`), then from `R2` (`CH1_IN`) to `U1` pin `3`.
  - Route `J6` pin `T` (`CH2_JACK_IN`) directly to `D4` (`PESD12VL1BA`), `R6` (`100K`), and `R7` (`1K`), then from `R7` (`CH2_IN`) to `U2` pin `3`.
  - Route `J10` pin `T` (`CH3_JACK_IN`) directly to `D5` (`PESD12VL1BA`), `R11` (`100K`), and `R12` (`1K`), then from `R12` (`CH3_IN`) to `U3` pin `3`.
  - Route `U1` pins `7`, `8`, `14` through `R3`, `R4`, `R5` to `J3`, `J4`, `J5` tip (`T`) pins.
  - Route `U2` pins `7`, `8`, `14` through `R8`, `R9`, `R10` to `J7`, `J8`, `J9` tip (`T`) pins.
  - Route `U3` pins `7`, `8`, `14` through `R13`, `R14`, `R15` to `J11`, `J12`, `J13` tip (`T`) pins.
- **Power Distribution (`+12V` & `-12V`)**:
  - Route `0.50 mm` traces from `J1` pins `9, 10` (`+12V_RAW`) -> `D1` Anode -> `D1` Cathode (`+12V`) -> `C1` pin `1` -> `C3`/`C5`/`C7` -> `U1`/`U2`/`U3` pin `4`.
  - Route `0.50 mm` traces from `J1` pins `1, 2` (`-12V_RAW`) -> `D2` Cathode -> `D2` Anode (`-12V`) -> `C2` pin `2` -> `C4`/`C6`/`C8` -> `U1`/`U2`/`U3` pin `11`.

### 3. Refill Zones & Add Stitching Vias
- Press **`B`** to refill both `GND` copper zones.
- Add stitching vias (`V` while routing on `GND`) connecting `F.Cu` and `B.Cu` ground pours near the op-amp decoupling capacitors (`C3`–`C8`) and along the board edges.

## 7. Design Rules Check (DRC) & Fabrication Export

1. **Run DRC**:
   - Open **`Inspect -> Design Rules Checker`** and click **Run DRC**.
   - Verify **0 Errors** and **0 Unconnected Items** (`J3`–`J5`, `J7`–`J9`, and `J11`–`J13` `TN` switch pins are marked no-connect in the netlist).
2. **Export Gerbers & Drill Files**:
   - Go to **`File -> Fabrication Outputs -> Gerbers (.gbr)...`**.
   - Plot layers: `F.Cu`, `B.Cu`, `F.Paste`, `B.Paste`, `F.Silkscreen`, `B.Silkscreen`, `F.Mask`, `B.Mask`, and `Edge.Cuts`.
   - Click **Generate Drill Files...** -> **Generate Drill File** (Excellon format, PTH and NPTH merged or separate per fab preference).
3. **Export Component Placement (CPL / POS) for JLCPCB SMT**:
   - Go to **`File -> Fabrication Outputs -> Component Placement (.pos)...`**.
   - Select **CSV**, **Millimeters**, and **Single file for board** (or **Back side only** since all 29 SMT parts are on `B.Cu`).
4. **Upload to JLCPCB**:
   - Upload the zipped Gerbers + Drill files.
   - Enable **PCB Assembly (Bottom Side)**.
   - Upload [`buffered_multiple_jlcpcb_bom.csv`](buffered_multiple_jlcpcb_bom.csv) (which includes only the 29 SMT components with verified LCSC part numbers and automatically excludes the hand-soldered THT jacks `J2`–`J13`, power header `J1`, and radial bulk caps `C1`–`C2`).
   - Upload the `.pos` placement file and verify the diode (`D1`, `D2`) cathode bands and op-amp (`U1`, `U2`, `U3`) pin-1 orientations in the 2D/3D preview.
