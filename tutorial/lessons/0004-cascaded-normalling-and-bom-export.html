1. How Three Lines of Python Create 1:3, 1:6, and 1:9 Routing

Because we designed buffered_channel_1to3(vcc, vee, gnd, norm_in, buffered_tap_out, ch_num) in Lesson 0003 to accept norm_in (wired to the input jack's Switched Tip Normal Contact (TN)) and buffered_tap_out (driven by Stage A's unity-gain output), assembling the entire 3-channel cascade in build_buffered_multiple() takes just three calls:

  1. Channel 1: buffered_channel_1to3(vcc, vee, gnd, gnd, ch1_buf, ch_num=1, tag="ch1") — When IN_1 (`J2`) is unpatched, its `TN` switch grounds the input to `0.00V` so none of the op-amp inputs float.
  2. Channel 2: buffered_channel_1to3(vcc, vee, gnd, ch1_buf, ch2_buf, ch_num=2, tag="ch2") — When IN_2 (`J6`) is unpatched, its `TN` switch receives ch1_buf (the buffered output of `U1A`, not the raw `IN_1` jack tip!), cascading Channels 1 and 2 into a zero-droop 1-to-6 multiple.
  3. Channel 3: buffered_channel_1to3(vcc, vee, gnd, ch2_buf, ch3_buf, ch_num=3, tag="ch3") — When IN_3 (`J10`) is unpatched, its `TN` switch receives ch2_buf (the buffered output of `U2A`), cascading all three channels into a 1-to-9 multiple—or splitting into `1:3 + 1:6` whenever a cable is patched into `IN_2`!

3. Assigning KiCad PCB Routing Rules with NetClass

In a mixed power-and-precision-CV layout like BufferedMult.kicad_pcb, your power rails (+12V, -12V, GND) should use wider copper traces (`0.5 mm`) and larger clearances (`0.25 mm`) than low-current analog signal nets (`0.25 mm` trace width, `0.2 mm` clearance). 4. Automated SMT-Filtered JLCPCB PCBA BOM Generation

To order assembled boards from JLCPCB without paying for manual through-hole soldering of the 12 jacks, 2 radial bulk caps, and 10-pin IDC header, generate_jlcpcb_bom() (lines 269–294 of buffered_multiple.py) iterates over builtins.default_circuit.parts and applies three transformations:

  1. Filter out THT parts by default: Skips any component without an LCSC entry in part.fields or with "THT" in part.fields.get("JLCPCB_Class", "") when include_tht=False.
  2. Strip the KiCad library nickname from footprints: getattr(part, "footprint", "").split(":")[-1] turns "Resistor_SMD:R_0603_1608Metric" into "R_0603_1608Metric" so JLCPCB's placement parser recognizes the package.
  3. Group identical `(comment, footprint, lcsc)` tuples: Combines all 9 output resistors (`R3, R4, R5, R8, R9, R10, R13, R14, R15`) into a single CSV row under JLCPCB's required 4-column header: Comment,Designator,Footprint,LCSC Part #.
