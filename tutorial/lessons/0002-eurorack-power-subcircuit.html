Growing the Script: Adding eurorack_power(vcc, vee, gnd)

Take our Stage 1 foundation (pre-import header + footprint and LCSC constants) and grow it into a working, ERC-verified Eurorack power supply script with a 10-pin Doepfer IDC header, 1N5819WS Schottky reverse-polarity protection, and 10uF bulk electrolytic capacitors.

Script Growth: Stage 1 → 155 Lines (5 Parts) ✓ ERC: 0 Errors, 0 Warnings ✓ Ruff: Clean

How @subcircuit Maps a Python Function to KiCad Hierarchy

In Lesson 0001, we grew our script to include the pre-import environment setup and our verified FP_* and LCSC_* constants. Now we will add our first hierarchical circuit block: @subcircuit def eurorack_power(vcc, vee, gnd):.

Decorating a function with SKiDL's Subcircuit Decorator (@subcircuit) does two important things:

  1. Interface Nets vs. Private Local Nets: The protected module rails (vcc, vee, gnd) are passed in as function arguments so downstream op-amp channels can attach to them later, while the raw header nets (vcc_raw = Net("+12V_RAW") and vee_raw = Net("-12V_RAW")) remain private inside eurorack_power.
  2. Deterministic Hierarchical Path Tags: When the top-level circuit calls eurorack_power(vcc, vee, gnd, tag="pwr") and each internal part defines a tag (such as tag="j_pwr", tag="d_prot", tag="c_bulk"), SKiDL hashes the hierarchical path (/pwr/c_bulk) into a deterministic KiCad tstamp UUID. Re-running your Python script never scrambles component associations in KiCad's PCB editor!

Step 1. Add the 10-Pin Eurorack IDC Header & Multi-Pin Slices

Instead of writing ten separate += statements for the 10-pin (`2×5`) Eurorack power header, SKiDL lets you index multiple pins in a single tuple subscript:

  • vee_raw += j_pwr[1, 2] wires pins 1 and 2 (the -12V red-stripe end) to -12V_RAW.
  • gnd += j_pwr[3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8] wires all six center ground pins to GND in one statement.
  • vcc_raw += j_pwr[9, 10] wires pins 9 and 10 to +12V_RAW.

Step 2. Add Bipolar Schottky Diode & Bulk Capacitor Polarity

Next, inside eurorack_power(), we add two 1N5819WS Schottky diodes (d_pos, d_neg) and two 10uF radial electrolytic bulk capacitors (c_bulk_pos, c_bulk_neg) cloned from templates. Expressing -12V diode and electrolytic capacitor orientation in code requires care with pin names ("A", "K") and numbers (1, 2):

Pin-by-pin polarity in eurorack_power(vcc, vee, gnd)
Component Rail SKiDL Wiring Electrical Reason
d_pos (D1, 1N5819WS) +12V vcc_raw += d_pos["A"]
vcc += d_pos["K"]		 Conventional current flows out of the header (+12V_RAW) into the Anode (A) and exits the Cathode (K) into the module's +12V rail.
d_neg (D2, 1N5819WS) -12V vee_raw += d_neg["K"]
vee += d_neg["A"]		 On the negative rail, conventional current returns from the module's -12V rail (vee) into the Anode (A) and exits the Cathode (K) back into the header (-12V_RAW).
c_bulk_pos (C1, 10uF) +12V to GND vcc += c_bulk_pos[1]
gnd += c_bulk_pos[2]		 +12V is positive relative to 0V, so pin 1 (+ anode) connects to vcc and pin 2 (- cathode) connects to gnd.
c_bulk_neg (C2, 10uF) GND to -12V gnd += c_bulk_neg[1]
vee += c_bulk_neg[2]		 0V (GND) sits at a higher potential than -12V! Therefore pin 1 (+ anode) must connect to gnd and pin 2 (- cathode) to vee.
