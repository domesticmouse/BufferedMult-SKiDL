Single 1-to-3 Buffered Channel (TL074H & Switched Jacks)

Build buffered_channel_1to3(vcc, vee, gnd, norm_in, buffered_tap_out, ch_num=1) around a single quad TL074H op-amp: wire input ESD/TVS clamping, Stage A input buffering, three loop-generated output followers with 470R series isolation, and builtins.NC on unused jack switches.

Anatomy of buffered_channel_1to3

Each 1-to-3 channel in buffered_multiple.py (lines 154–267) maps all four internal operational amplifiers of one TL074H (SOIC-14) package to a clean two-tier buffer hierarchy:

  • Stage A (Input Follower, Pins 1, 2, 3): Presents a high-impedance load (`100K` pull-down) to the patched CV/audio source and produces a low-impedance buffered signal on buffered_tap_out.
  • Stages B, C, D (Output Followers, Pins 5–14): Three independent unity-gain followers driven by buffered_tap_out, each driving its own output jack through a 470R series protection resistor so a short circuit or cable capacitance on one output never disturbs the other two outputs—or any downstream normalled channel.

1. Two-Pin Series Networks with the & Operator

For any two-pin passive component (resistors, ceramic capacitors, bidirectional TVS diodes), SKiDL overloads Python's bitwise AND operator (&) to wire components in series between nets in a single readable expression:

  • vcc & c_dec_pos & gnd and gnd & c_dec_neg & vee wire the two local 100nF ceramic decoupling capacitors (`C3`, `C4`) from the op-amp rails to ground.
  • in_jack & d_tvs & gnd wires the PESD12VL1BA bidirectional TVS diode (`D3`) from the input jack tip to ground.
  • in_jack & r_pd & gnd wires the 100K input pull-down resistor (`R1`) to ground.
  • in_jack & r_in & in_sig wires the 1K series current-limiting resistor (`R2`) between the raw jack tip (CH1_JACK_IN) and Stage A's non-inverting input net (CH1_IN).

2. Loop-Driven Op-Amp Stage Wiring & Tuple Pin Attachment

Instead of copy-pasting identical schematic blocks three times for Outputs A, B, and C, Python lets us express Stages B, C, and D as a compact data table of (suffix, pin_pos, pin_neg, pin_out) tuples and iterate over it:

TL074H (SOIC-14) unit pin assignments in buffered_channel_1to3
Op-Amp Stage Role Non-Inverting (+) Inverting (-) Output Unity-Gain Feedback Expression
Unit A Input Buffer u.p[3] u.p[2] u.p[1] buffered_tap_out += u.p[1], u.p[2]
Unit B ("A") Output Buffer A u.p[5] u.p[6] u.p[7] op_out += u.p[7], u.p[6]
Unit C ("B") Output Buffer B u.p[10] u.p[9] u.p[8] op_out += u.p[8], u.p[9]
Unit D ("C") Output Buffer C u.p[12] u.p[13] u.p[14] op_out += u.p[14], u.p[13]

Notice how buffered_tap_out += u.p[1], u.p[2] attaches both the op-amp output (`pin 1`) and the inverting input (`pin 2`) to buffered_tap_out at once—creating the unity-gain feedback loop AND feeding the downstream output stages (`u.p[5]`, `u.p[10]`, `u.p[12]`) on the same net!

Why Output Jacks Use j_out["TN"] += builtins.NC (Pin on the Left)

All 12 jacks on the board use the same physical part (WQP-PJ398SM / AudioJack2_SwitchT) to simplify purchasing and panel assembly. On the 9 output jacks, the Switched Tip Normal Contact (TN) is unused. In SKiDL, the No-Connect Net (builtins.NC) is injected into Python's builtins module at import time and is not exported in skidl.__all__ (so from skidl import NC raises ImportError). Furthermore, inside a function or @subcircuit, writing builtins.NC += j_out["TN"] makes Python treat builtins.NC as a reassigned local variable; always place the pin on the left: j_out["TN"] += builtins.NC.
